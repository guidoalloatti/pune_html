How to play

Survive longer than everyone else. Keep turning to avoid trails, collect points, and reach the target score first.

Quick Start
  • Local: pick at least 2 worms, assign left/right keys, then press “Create a game.”
  • Online: create or join a room, then “Ready Up.” The host starts the match.
  • Mobile: use the on-screen left/right buttons and press-and-hold to steer.
Controls
  • Each worm has its own left/right keys to steer continuously.
  • Tap Pause to freeze the action and bring up overlays.
Scoring & Rounds
  • Last worm alive scores a point each round.
  • First to the target score wins the match.
  • If Hole Points are enabled, passing holes can score too.
Gameplay Tips
  • Speed can ramp up randomly—plan wider turns early.
  • Use holes to slip through tight situations without crashing.
  • Spectators can switch in and out anytime in online rooms.

About Pune

      
    

    

      
Creator: Guido Alloatti

      
Pune is a fast-paced, multiplayer light‑cycle game built for local and online play. It blends classic snake-style movement with modern UI, holes, and speed boosts for chaotic rounds.

      
Thanks for playing and sharing feedback — it helps make the game sharper and more fun.
